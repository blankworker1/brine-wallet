BRINE.
BITCOIN UTXO OCEAN

Most Bitcoin wallets show you a balance. BRINE shows you something more important: whether your funds can actually move.

Every satoshi you own exists as a UTXO — an unspent output locked to your address. To spend it, you pay a mining fee. If that fee exceeds the UTXO's value, the sat is effectively trapped. BRINE calls this Salt.

Paste your extended public key and BRINE maps your entire wallet onto the ocean — placing each UTXO at its position in the density hierarchy, against a live fee-pressure sieve. Works with BIP84 (zpub), BIP49 (ypub), and BIP44 (xpub) wallets.

The Sieve. Mining fees create a pressure threshold. Any UTXO too small to cover its own transaction cost becomes Salt — it exists on-chain but cannot move. The sieve line shifts with every block. BRINE tracks it live from the mempool.
1
Scan or paste your xpub / zpub. Export it from your wallet as a QR code or copy the text. Your key never leaves your browser.
2
BRINE derives your addresses and queries the Bitcoin mempool for every UTXO, live.
3
Your ocean appears. Each dot is a UTXO. Green is liquid. Amber is in the sieve zone. Red is Salt. The threshold line moves in real time with network fees.
